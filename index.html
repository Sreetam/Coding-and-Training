High-Quality Resources for DSA, Aptitude, and Interview Preparation

For CS/IT students at Jadavpur University preparing for roles in FAANG, quant firms, startups, and consulting, below is a comprehensive list of top-notch training resources. The list is organized by category and includes GitHub repositories, websites, YouTube channels, and blogs. Each resource is described with its focus and indicated level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).

Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)

  • Ravindrababu Ravula – Algorithms Playlist (YouTube) – Clear and detailed lectures on algorithms, ideal for GATE and interviews. (Intermediate)
  • GeeksforGeeks – One of the most popular sites for learning DSA and core CS topics. (Beginner to Intermediate)
  • LeetCode – A leading platform for practicing coding problems with editorials. (Intermediate)
  • takeUforward (YouTube) – Curated playlists on DSA and interview questions by a Google SDE. (Beginner to Intermediate)
  • NeetCode (YouTube) – Walkthroughs of LeetCode problems with clear explanations. (Intermediate)
  • Abdul Bari (YouTube) – Clear algorithm lectures using whiteboard-style teaching. (Beginner)
  • The Algorithms (GitHub) – A repository of classic algorithms in multiple programming languages. (Intermediate)
  • Programiz – Beginner-friendly tutorials on data structures and algorithms. (Beginner)
  • MIT OpenCourseWare 6.006 – MIT’s free course on algorithms. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • Coding Interview University (GitHub) – A complete self-study CS curriculum for aspiring software engineers. (Beginner to Advanced)
  • Tech Interview Handbook (GitHub) – A highly structured guide to technical and behavioral interview prep. (Intermediate)
  • LeetCode Patterns (GitHub) – Interview problem sets grouped by common algorithmic patterns. (Intermediate)
  • JavaScript Algorithms (GitHub) – DSA implementations with readable explanations in JavaScript. (Intermediate)
  • labuladong’s Algorithm Book (GitHub) – Deep dives into dynamic programming and hard problems with intuition-focused guides. (Advanced)
  • William Fiset (YouTube) – Detailed tutorials on advanced data structures like tries, segment trees, etc. (Advanced)
  • Back To Back SWE (YouTube) – Top-tier problem walkthroughs with whiteboard-style explanations. (Intermediate)
  • Nick White (YouTube) – Real-time coding of LeetCode-style interview problems. (Beginner to Intermediate)
  • Tushar Roy (YouTube) – Step-by-step tutorials on popular algorithms and design problems. (Intermediate)
  • Cracking FAANG (YouTube) – Interview-focused DSA content from a Meta engineer. (Intermediate)

Quantitative Aptitude and Logical Reasoning

  • IndiaBix – Bank of aptitude and logical reasoning problems with solutions. (Beginner to Intermediate)
  • GeeksforGeeks – Aptitude – Step-by-step aptitude tutorials and quizzes. (Beginner to Intermediate)
  • Brilliant.org – Interactive platform for math and logic skills. (Beginner to Advanced)
  • CareerRide (YouTube) – Concise video tutorials on aptitude and reasoning. (Beginner)
  • 5-Minute Engineering (YouTube) – Short videos on aptitude topics. (Beginner)
  • Jane Street Puzzle Archive – Monthly puzzles and brainteasers from a trading firm. (Intermediate)

Interview Preparation (Behavioral + Technical)

  • Cracking the Coding Interview (Book) – 200+ coding and behavioral questions with STAR approach. (Intermediate)
  • Tech Interview Handbook – In-depth guide to coding and behavioral interviews. (Intermediate)
  • Coding Interview University (GitHub) – Self-taught CS interview prep roadmap. (Beginner to Advanced)
  • InterviewBit – Gamified platform for coding, puzzles, and SQL prep. (Intermediate)
  • System Design Primer (GitHub) – Concepts and patterns for system design interviews. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • Pramp – Peer-to-peer live mock interviews. (Intermediate)
  • Glassdoor – Interview questions and reviews by company. (Intermediate)
  • ByteByteGo (YouTube) – Animated system design walkthroughs by Alex Xu. (Intermediate)

Quant/Finance Specific Prep

  • A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews by Xinfeng Zhou – Collection of quant puzzles and math questions. (Advanced)
  • Heard on The Street by Timothy Crack – Brain teasers and quant finance questions. (Advanced)
  • Jane Street – Markets Guide – Probability and market-making intro. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • Interview Primer for Quant Finance (GitHub) – Technical interview prep for quant roles. (Advanced)
  • Project Euler – Programming problems requiring math and efficient algorithms. (Advanced)
  • Zetamac Mental Math Tool – Speed math drills for quant interviews. (Intermediate)
  • StackExchange Puzzles – Community of logic puzzles and brainteasers. (Intermediate to Advanced)

General Competitive Programming

  • Codeforces – Contests and problem archive with global community. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • CodeChef – Competitive programming platform with practice series. (Beginner to Advanced)
  • AtCoder – Japanese contest platform with math-focused problems. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • TopCoder – Classic SRMs and competitive programming legacy. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • CP-Algorithms – Theory and implementations for competitive programming. (Intermediate to Advanced)
  • USACO Guide – Structured curriculum for competitive programming. (Beginner to Advanced)
  • HackerRank – Interview kits and problem categories. (Beginner to Intermediate)
  • Codeforces EDU – Structured lessons and topic-based problem sets. (Intermediate)

Each of these resources is highly regarded in its category. By leveraging a mix of these, students can cover all aspects of interview and competitive exam preparation – from coding and algorithms to aptitude, puzzles, and system design.

Happy learning and coding!
